ALWAYS-ON JOB-SEARCH INTELLIGENCE

Less noise.
More momentum.

Your job search is a connected process.
Your tools should work that way, too.

Most tools help with one step. RoleDaemon is being built to connect the whole loop: discovering opportunities, understanding fit, researching the people behind them, and keeping the next action clear.

WHY “DAEMON”?

A daemon for the
work behind the work.

In computing, a daemon is a process that keeps working in the background. That’s the idea behind RoleDaemon: a persistent companion watching the market and bringing useful context forward.

Automate the busywork.
Keep the important decisions human.

HOW IT WORKS

One search. A continuous loop.

The product direction, from first signal to next step.

  1. 01

    Discover

    Find the signal.

    Bring promising opportunities into one view, without repeating the same searches.

    Search listings. Receive alerts.
  2. 02

    Evaluate

    Understand the fit.

    Look beyond the title. Put strengths, gaps, and company context beside each role.

    Score job fit. Research companies.
  3. 03

    Connect

    Make the next move.

    Connect people, research, and application history to the opportunity.

    Find hiring managers. Track applications.
  4. 04

    Pursue

    Keep the momentum.

    Prepare thoughtfully and keep the next action in sight. You review what goes out.

    Tailor resumes. Schedule follow-ups.

INTELLIGENCE, NOT JUST AUTOMATION

Show the reasons.
Not just the score.

A number is a starting point. The useful part is understanding why a role fits, where the gaps are, and what you still need to find out.

  1. 01

    Your direction comes first.

    Experience, goals, location, and constraints give the search its context.

  2. 02

    Bring the evidence together.

    Role details, company research, and people context belong beside the opportunity.

  3. 03

    Make uncertainty visible.

    The design calls for clear reasons and open questions, so you can decide what deserves attention.
